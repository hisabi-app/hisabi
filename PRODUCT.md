# Product

## Register

product

## Users
The owner and their household, plus open-source self-hosters who find Hisabi on GitHub, try the demo, and run it on their own server. They use it in three places, about equally:
- On a phone, for quick checks after a bank SMS arrives ("what did that cost, how much is left in groceries").
- On a laptop, sitting down to review the month, fix uncategorized brands, and adjust budgets.
- On a tablet mounted on the kitchen wall (`/kiosk`), read at a glance from across the room.

The job: know where the money went this month, whether spending is on pace, and keep the data tidy with as little effort as possible.

## Product Purpose
A self-hosted, single-user personal finance tracker that turns bank SMS messages into transactions and shows what matters about them. Success is a person trusting the numbers, understanding them at a glance, and acting on them (cutting back on a category, fixing a brand) without needing explanations.

## Brand Personality
Calm, precise, warm. It should feel like a well-kept account book that belongs to you: quiet and private, exact about numbers, and human in how it talks. Confidence comes from clarity, not decoration.

## Anti-references
- Default shadcn / generic SaaS template (the current look).
- Bank apps: navy and gold, corporate.
- Crypto and trading apps: neon on black.
- Cutesy budgeting apps: mascots, confetti, gamification.
- Dense admin dashboards: walls of cards, tiles and charts.
- Tinted or colored backgrounds. The canvas is always white or off-white; color lives in the content.

## Design Principles
1. **Numbers first.** Amounts are the content. They get the best typography, alignment and contrast on every screen.
2. **Only what you will act on.** No derived stats, projections or summary tiles just because the data allows them. If a number needs a sentence of explanation, it doesn't belong.
3. **Quiet by default, loud on purpose.** Color and emphasis are reserved for what needs attention (over budget, uncategorized, needs review).
4. **One system, three distances.** The same identity has to work on a phone in hand, a laptop at a desk, and a wall tablet across the room.
5. **Yours, not a bank's.** Private and personal in tone: plain language, no corporate gloss, no marketing voice inside the app.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA contrast in both light and dark themes. Never encode money state (over/under, income/expense) by red/green color alone; pair it with a sign, label or shape. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Kiosk numbers must be legible from a few metres away.
